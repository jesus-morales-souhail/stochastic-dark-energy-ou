1. BAO ANALYSIS — ORNSTEIN–UHLENBECK + QNM MODELS (DESI DR2)
================================================================================

Data: DESI DR2 BAO (arXiv:2503.14738) — 7 redshift bins
      Public summary statistics; measurement cov = DESI Gaussian BAO 13x13 projected to alpha (block-diagonal across bins).

Background (CPL, BAO-only):
  w0 ≈ −1.001
  wa ≈ +0.014
  (close to flat ΛCDM under the fiducial used for α = 1)

Models tested on residuals:
- Baseline: ΛCDM (same projected 13x13 covariance)
- H0: Stationary Ornstein–Uhlenbeck (OU) fluctuations
- H1: Damped oscillatory QNM kernel

Results:

  OU model (H0):
    θ     = 0.001  (≈ boundary, effectively undamped)
    σ_X   = 5 × 10⁻⁵   (old run, diagonal cov: archive/results/ou_bao_desi_dr2_run.txt)
    Δlogℒ vs ΛCDM = 0.00
    AIC = -50.03 | BIC = -50.14

  QNM model (H1):
    θ     = 0.001
    σ_X   = 5 × 10⁻⁵   (old run, diagonal cov)
    ω_R   = 0.00
    Δlogℒ vs ΛCDM = 0.00
    AIC = -48.03 | BIC = -48.19

Conclusion:
- No statistical evidence for stochastic fluctuations in the dark energy sector.
- The BAO summary statistics are consistent with a smooth background near ΛCDM.
- 95% CL upper limit: σ_X < 2.6 × 10⁻² (profile over θ, background fixed at α = 1)
- The stochastic amplitude is driven to zero when the background is free.
  Nested CPL + (σ, θ) extensions are not preferred (ΔAIC ≈ +4).

1b. CMB CONSISTENCY (Planck 2018, analisis_cmb/)
- 200 OU realizations at sigma_X = 2.633e-2, H0 solved for theta_*: p95 dchi2 TT = 0.067, phiphi = 0.054 (theta = 1); 0 with dchi2 > 4.
- Grid 2.506e-2 -> 1e-4: p95 < 4 everywhere, linear in sigma_X. Planck does not bound sigma_X below DESI.
- sigma(H0) = 0.48 km/s/Mpc (theta = 1), 0.020 (theta = 0.001): 12x and 280x below 73.04 - 67.36 = 5.68.
- Harmonic X = A sin(2pi ln a / P + phi), A <= 0.025: 58/60 allowed by DESI BAO, 0/60 distinguishable in the CMB.

3. STOCHASTIC DARK ENERGY MODEL STATUS
================================================================================

Main result:
The stationary stochastic model (Ornstein–Uhlenbeck + QNM) is **not favored** by DESI DR2 BAO data.

- When the background (w0, wa) is left free (results/joint_w0wa_sigma), the stochastic amplitude is consistent with zero.
- The 95% CL bound is a profile over θ with the background fixed at α = 1: an upper limit, not a detection.
- The result supports the working hypothesis of **Late-Time Vacuum Homogeneity** at currently observable scales.
- The Planck 2018 CMB adds no constraint at this amplitude (section 1b).

Future tests:
- The full Euclid DR1 release with cosmology products (expected mid-2027; the November 2026 DR1-Foundation release has no LE3 products) should provide many more BAO bins than DESI DR2 (design assumption: no Euclid binning published), with sensitivity aimed at σ_X ∼ 10⁻⁵ (forecast target, not a measurement).
- The current analysis pipeline is ready to be applied to upcoming datasets.
- The CMB gives no extra leverage on sigma_X at the DESI ceiling; BAO remains the test.
